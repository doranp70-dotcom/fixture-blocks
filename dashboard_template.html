Method. Sky Bet outright odds (title, relegation, top-4 / promotion, 10–14 Aug 2026) and, for the Premier League, Spreadex season points lines (28 Jul) are turned into a strength rating per team in a Dixon-Coles Poisson goals model, fitted so that a 20,000-run simulation of the real fixture list reproduces the market. Each fixture's market expected points = 3 × P(win) + P(draw); that number is frozen for the season. In-season, attack and defence are re-rated on match xG (OddAlerts / Sportmonks, all four divisions) around the August prior, and the rest of the season is simulated 10,000 times from the live table to price every outright market against bet365. Southampton's −4 is applied to the table. Man City's 8/1 relegation price is treated as a points-deduction price and ignored in the fit.

Blocks follow each team's own kick-off order; a game that moves block is flagged. A block's +/- compares points won with market xP for the games played so far in that block. z is that difference in standard deviations given the variance of those fixtures.
